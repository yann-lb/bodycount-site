BodyCount

A number. Not a judgment.

A little black book of everyone you’ve met, stats that actually tell you something, badges to unlock, and a health side that takes things seriously. All on your phone, encrypted, with no account and no server.

The fun side

One profile per person, a home screen you arrange yourself, stats in three tabs, a first-name bingo, challenges, and cards to share.

The health side

Hookups, STI tests and vaccines, with reminders based on French health guidelines — and their sources, right there in the app.

Nothing leaves your phone

No account, no server, no ads. On Android, the app doesn’t even have permission to access the internet.

The idea

BodyCount doesn’t rate anyone and doesn’t lecture anyone. The point is to take the shame out of how we think about sex: count, remember, look after yourself. The app points you to official guidelines; it doesn’t prescribe anything. For anything about your own health, a healthcare professional is the one to talk to.

The app itself is in French for now.
